I · Digital twins

Can a large language model given a person's profile reproduce that person's survey answers? Where it succeeds, it is a measurement tool. Where it fails, the failure is the finding. The mechanism studies ask what the profile changes inside the model.

II · Simulation validity

The same question for a whole population: do agents built on large language models reproduce social processes, or only their outcomes? A simulation can match the outcome while the process behind it is wrong.

III · Representativeness

My dissertation, The Expressed Public (2026). Inferring demographics and stance from social media posts, and asking whom that expression represents. Published work on census self-completion, stance annotation and echo chambers belongs to this thread.

IV · Survey instruments

AI interviewers, and coding of open-ended survey answers with large language models, built and evaluated as survey instruments.

V · Text structuring

Methods the other threads rely on: fast ranking and summarization of large collections of posts.
